# OpenVK Coin bot

---

[{github} Source code](https://github.com/ZAZiOs/ovkcoin-tg-bot)

---

[OpenVK Coin bot](https://t.me/ovkcbot) - A Telegram wallet bot for the OVK Coin online currency. It was actively used by the game's community. Currently, it is disabled and archived due to the cessation of support for the OVK Coin game.

<img src="/mdcontent/ovkcoin1.png" alt="OpenVK Coin example">

---

## Core Functionality:
- Linking an OpenVK Coin account to the Telegram bot.
- Creating and activating in-game balance checks.
- Checks split for multiple uses.
- Password-protected checks.

---

## Tech Stack:
- Telegraf.js
- Express.js (Authorization Backend)
- MariaDB
