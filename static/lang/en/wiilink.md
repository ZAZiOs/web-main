I participated in the international fan project [**WiiLink**](https://wiilink.ca/), which is dedicated to restoring and modernizing the lost online services of the Nintendo Wii.
My role involved localizing Wii Channels and adapting server components to support the Russian language.

### Localization of Wii Channels

Performed the translation and adaptation of a range of services:

* **Forecast Channel** — full translation of the interface, texts, UI elements, graphics, including modifications to assembly code and server-side parts in some places. (Not completed due to team difficulties. Planned for completion once work on the channel resumes).
* **Everybody Votes Channel** — translation of strings, graphical elements, text templates, and system messages. (As well as polls for the initial launch period)
* **Wii Room (Wii no Ma)** — full interface localization.

The work involved translation, drawing new graphical assets, adapting strings for the original interface's limitations, modifying source code within the disassembly, and integration into the channel format.

### Server-Side Localization

In addition to translating the interface, I also worked on adapting the **server-side components** to ensure the channels correctly displayed Russian data.

For example:
* For the **Forecast Channel**, I localized server responses so that temperature, weather descriptions, city names, and forecasts would display correctly in Russian.
* For the **Everybody Votes Channel**, I added support for the Russian region and Russian polls.

### Contribution to the WiiLink Community

My work allowed Russian-speaking users to fully use the restored Wii Channels for the first time, as it would have appeared in an official localization.
