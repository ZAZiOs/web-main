# OpenVK Native

---

[{github} Source code](https://github.com/ZAZiOs/OpenVK-Native/tree/vue-experimental) &nbsp;&nbsp; [{android} Download](https://t.me/openvkmobile/17850/18618)

---

**OpenVK Native** is a mobile client for the OpenVK social network. The project was created to simplify access to OpenVK from mobile devices, as the web version is not always convenient. A public beta is currently available, with iOS builds planned for the future.

---

## Implemented Functionality:
- Viewing the global feed
- Viewing your personal feed
- Viewing your wall, other walls, groups, and comments.
- HTTP/HTTPS support, 2FA.
- Opening ovk.to links

## Planned Features:
- Full access to the media library.
- Access to social interactions (Friends list, Groups list)
- In the future, after the messenger update, its support will also be added.
