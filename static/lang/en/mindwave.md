## Localization of **Mindwave** (Cozy Inn) — *Planned*

I am a member of the [**Cozy Inn**](https://t.me/cozy_inn) team, which plans to work on localizing the game **Mindwave** *(after completing the Deltarune translation, as of November 19, 2025)*. I have already made a small initial contribution to its Russification, and in the future, I plan to handle the full technical and visual cycle of localization—similar to my role in the **Deltarune** translation.

### Current Contribution

So far, I have taken the first preparatory steps for the localization:
* basic analysis of the game's structure and possibilities for localization integration;
* experimental adaptation of graphics;
* translation and integration of one of the bosses;
* studying technical limitations and resource formats.

This has laid the groundwork for further full-scale work on the localization.
