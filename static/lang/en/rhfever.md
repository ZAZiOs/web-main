## Localization Patch for **Rhythm Heaven Fever** (Rhythm Heaven Wii)

---

[{youtube} Video "The Challenges of Translating Rhythm Heaven Fever" in Russian](https://youtu.be/09p0Rt978q8)

---

I am the author of the localization patch for **Rhythm Heaven Fever** on the **Nintendo Wii**, serving as the project organizer and lead technical specialist. The work covered all areas except for the text translation, which was handled by invited translators. Below are the main roles I performed in the project:

### Project Organization

Fully organized the localization patch development process:
* coordinated the work of the translation team;
* assigned tasks and deadlines;
* handled quality control and the final integration of all localization elements.

### Technical Game Localization

Responsible for all technical aspects of implementing the Russian localization:
* analyzing and modifying Wii game files;
* replacing and adjusting in-game resources;
* complete integration of Russian fonts, UI, and graphics;
* fixing compatibility issues and errors arising from the switch to Cyrillic.

### Sound Design

Handled the organization, sourcing of vocals, and mixing of cover songs for the game.

### Translation and Adaptation of Graphics

Performed a full translation of the game's graphical elements:
* redrawing the logo, UI, and fonts;
* adapting HUD elements and mini-game interfaces;
* working with raster and vector graphics to preserve the original style.
