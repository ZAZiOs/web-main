# Cat-Manager (Bot)

---

[{github} Source code](https://github.com/ZAZiOs/comment-watchbot-tg/) &nbsp;&nbsp; [{telegram} Telegram bot](https://t.me/UltraKicker3000bot)

---

**Cat-Manager** — a minimalist watchdog bot for Telegram comment chats.
Its task is to maintain order as meticulously as a strict watchman would:

- **Deletes any NON-comments** sent by non-admins and not under a channel post.
- **Instantly kicks all new members** who do not meet the access rules (not an admin/not on the ignore list).
- Runs reliably, requires no complex setup, and is lightweight.

---

Built using [Telegraf.js](https://telegraf.js.org/)
