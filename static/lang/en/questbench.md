# Questbench

##### *"Bring your story to life"*

**NOTE:** *This project was completed only at an early stage; its full version is merely a concept, as presented here.*

**Questbench** is a modern online platform that allows you to create interactive quests and visual novels without unnecessary complexity. Inspired by the idea of fanfiction on Ficbook, the project enables every user to create their unique stories and share them with the world.

---

## Core Features

- **Simple Story Creation** — An intuitive interface lets you focus on the plot, not technical details.  
- **Basic Lua Support** — For working with variables, logic, and interactivity.  
- **Online Publishing** — Finished quests can be shared with friends and a wide audience.  
- **Complete Creative Freedom** — Create your own worlds, characters, and unique scenarios.

---

## Project Goal

To give everyone the ability to easily create visual novels and interactive stories, fostering creativity and sharing ideas with others.  

---

## Target Audience

- Fans of interactive stories  
- Creators and writers who want to bring their ideas to life  
- Beginners interested in the world of visual novels without complex programming
