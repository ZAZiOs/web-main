Hello! My name is Daniel, aka ZAZA. I'm a versatile developer who can adapt to almost any task: from frontend and backend to mobile apps, game development, and DevOps.
My primary profession is *DevOps Engineer*, but my activities aren't limited to this role. I love exploring, creating, improving, and bringing projects to life :)

### My Main Focus

A special place in my heart is reserved for **Russian language localization**. The process itself captivates me: when no one provides you with ready-made files, you have to reverse-engineer to extract strings, fonts, and graphics, making a project understandable to a wider audience. There's a certain vibe to it... like digital archaeology.

### What Inspires Me

My main source of inspiration is, without a doubt, music. It fills me with ideas, sets the rhythm, and helps me find a balance between emotion and cold, hard technique.
If I had to describe what I strive for in three words: Pleasantness, convenience, and perfectionism.

### My Approach

I try to structure my work to avoid chaos and allow for growth. The structure I create always evolves naturally, finding its final form over time.
