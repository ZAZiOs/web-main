I participated in the maintenance and development of the production server infrastructure for **Daniel Myslivets**, performing the tasks of a system administrator and DevOps engineer. As part of this work, I:

- **Configured and maintained Proxmox VE**, including managing virtual machines, LXC containers, network configuration, and storage.
- **Assisted in configuring the container infrastructure**, optimizing resource allocation and ensuring stable operation of services.
- **Implemented port forwarding and proxying through a web-proxy container**, including setting up reverse proxy and routing for external access to services.
- **Deployed and configured third-party services**, such as:
  - *Excalidraw* (collaborative drawing board)
  - *Outline* (documentation engine)
  - and other tools necessary for the team's work.

My role involved troubleshooting technical issues, improving service stability, and expanding the infrastructure to meet new production needs.
