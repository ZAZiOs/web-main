## Ludum Dare 58 — **Seal The Deal**

---

[{github} Source Code](https://github.com/xRy0/LudumDare58) &nbsp;&nbsp; [{controller} Itch.io](https://sasikqq.itch.io/seal-the-deal) &nbsp;&nbsp; [Ludum Dare page](https://ldjam.com/events/ludum-dare/58/seal-the-deal)

---

I participated in the creation of the game **Seal The Deal** for the **Ludum Dare 58** game jam, working in a team of three led by [**Sasikqq**](https://bento.me/sasik). My contribution covered programming, graphics, and all audio work. Below are the main areas I was responsible for in the project.

### Programming

During development, I worked on the technical aspects of the game in **Godot**, performing:
* fixes and bug elimination;
* implementation of small features and auxiliary systems;
* ensuring the correct operation of gameplay mechanics and UI.

*The main part of the code was written by [Ry0](https://ry0.ru)*

### Graphics

Although the main visual part was handled by Sasikqq, I contributed to drawing individual graphical elements:
* creation of several items;
* background art;
* worked in Aseprite.

### Sound Design

I was fully responsible for the audio component of the game:
* creation and processing of all sound effects;
* creation of accompanying music and the overall feel of the game.
  *(I believe a huge part of perception lies in the music)*

### Game Jam Results

* **Overall:** 254th place (3.685, 67 ratings)
* **Fun:** 309th place (3.477)
* **Innovation:** 352nd place (3.315)
* **Theme:** 155th place (4.069)
* **Graphics:** 405th place (3.631)
* **Audio:** 242nd place (3.532)
* **Humor:** 263rd place (3.339)
* **Mood:** 366th place (3.587)
